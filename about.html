Our Brief Heritage

Founded with a strong vision to reform basic educational development, **De Amazing International Group of Schools** has grown steadily into a top-tier learning institution in Nigeria. We balance instructional rigour with character development.

Our educational group caters to a diverse student body, preparing them to seamlessly pass standard examinations while developing the core competence needed for independent research, entrepreneurship, and technical growth.

🎯 Our Mission Statement

To deliver a well-rounded and globally relevant educational experience that enables children to unleash creative capacity, achieve moral uprightness, and attain elite scholarly recognition.

👁️ Our Shared Vision

To become the premier hub for transforming young minds into highly resourceful, reliable global leaders who shape future socioeconomic ecosystems across Africa and the world.
